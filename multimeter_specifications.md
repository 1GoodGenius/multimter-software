# Digital Multimeter Specifications & Product Descriptions

## Executive Summary
Professional digital multimeters designed for electrical testing and diagnostics. These versatile instruments provide accurate measurements for voltage, current, resistance, and additional electrical parameters.

---

## Core Technical Specifications

### Measurement Functions

#### 1. Voltage Measurement
- **DC Voltage Range**: 0.1mV to 1000V
  - Ranges: 200mV, 2V, 20V, 200V, 1000V
  - Accuracy: ±(0.5% + 2 digits)
  - Input Impedance: 10MΩ

- **AC Voltage Range**: 0.1V to 750V
  - Ranges: 2V, 20V, 200V, 750V
  - Accuracy: ±(1.0% + 3 digits)
  - Frequency Response: 45Hz to 1kHz

#### 2. Current Measurement
- **DC Current Range**: 10µA to 10A
  - Ranges: 2mA, 20mA, 200mA, 10A
  - Accuracy: ±(1.0% + 2 digits)
  - Burden Voltage: <200mV

- **AC Current Range**: 10µA to 10A
  - Ranges: 2mA, 20mA, 200mA, 10A
  - Accuracy: ±(1.5% + 3 digits)
  - True RMS Measurement (optional)

#### 3. Resistance Measurement
- **Range**: 0.1Ω to 40MΩ
  - Ranges: 200Ω, 2kΩ, 20kΩ, 200kΩ, 2MΩ, 20MΩ, 40MΩ
  - Accuracy: ±(0.8% + 2 digits)
  - Test Current: <0.7mA

#### 4. Additional Functions
- **Continuity Test**: Beeper indication <50Ω
- **Diode Test**: Forward voltage drop 0-2V
- **Frequency Measurement**: 1Hz to 10MHz
- **Capacitance Measurement**: 1pF to 100µF
- **Temperature Measurement**: -40°C to 1000°C (with thermocouple)
- **Duty Cycle**: 0.1% to 99.9%

## Display & Interface Features

### Display Specifications
- **Type**: Large LCD Display (3.5" or 4.5" digit)
- **Count**: 4000 or 6000 count display
- **Backlight**: White LED backlight with auto-off
- **Update Rate**: 2-3 readings per second
- **Bar Graph**: Analog bar graph display
- **Indicators**: Function symbols, battery status, warnings

### User Interface
- **Rotary Switch**: 30-position function/range selector
- **Buttons**: HOLD, REL, RANGE, BACKLIGHT
- **Auto Range**: Automatic range selection
- **Manual Override**: Manual range selection available
- **Data Hold**: Freeze reading on display

## Safety Features

### Electrical Safety
- **Safety Rating**: CAT III 600V, CAT II 1000V
- **Overload Protection**: All ranges protected
- **Fuse Protection**: 10A/250V and 500mA/250V fuses
- **Input Protection**: PTC resettable fuses

### User Safety
- **Double Insulation**: IEC 61010-1 compliant
- **Safety Rated Test Leads**: CAT III 600V rated
- **Warning Indicators**: High voltage, overload warnings
